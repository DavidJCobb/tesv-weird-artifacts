
[size=5][b]Weird Artifacts[/b][/size]

A new merchant has shown up in Skyrim, hanging around [url=https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Skyrim:Whitewatch_Tower]Whitewatch Tower[/url] in Whiterun Hold. Khahana, a Khajiit, sells a variety of strange magical artifacts that come into her possession.


[size=4][b]Artifact list[/b][/size]

[b]Items:[/b]
[spoiler]

[b]Blazing Hood:[/b] An enchanted hood. Doubles the amount of Spiky Grass you harvest, but prevents you from harvesting any other ingredients. Boosts fire and poison resistance by 20%, and reduces max stamina.

[b]Boots of the Rain Dancer:[/b] When the wearer enters combat, the weather will turn rainy.

[b]Cheese Strategist's Helm:[/b] This helmet offers its wearer a strange sort of protection: when the wearer's health drops low enough, they are given the means to restore it.

[b]Compass Arrows:[/b] Arrows that unleash a blast of light due north from the impact site.

[b]Dustbane:[/b] An enchanted broom that emits a powerful blast of wind, staggering anyone in its path.

[b]Fidgeter's Quill:[/b] Slowly restores your magicka while you're [url=https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/OnGrab_-_ObjectReference]fidgeting with it[/url].

[b]Inverted Lockpick:[/b] A strange lockpick; when held in your hand, you can interact with doors and containers to lock them.

[b]Item Box:[/b] A strange, crystalline box. Once dropped, it can be smashed open to produce a random item.

[b]Never-Melting Snowball:[/b] A throwable item (in the Scrolls menu) that can be thrown at hostile targets to deal an extremely small amount of frost damage. When thrown at non-hostile targets, it deals no damage and provokes no aggro.

[b]Pocket Sand:[/b] A throwable item (in the Scrolls menu) that fully blinds targets within a short range.

[b]Ponderer's Spoon:[/b] When used from the inventory, causes you to kneel down and meditate. This effect is more easily seen in third-person view.

[b]Ring of Thresholds:[/b] An enchanted ring. When you wear it, you can open doors but can no longer close them.

[b]Ring of a Ruler's Radiance:[/b] An enchanted ring that grants the wearer all the perks and privileges of being a ruler.

[b]Scroll of Second Chances:[/b] A mysterious scroll that grants the caster a second chance at their endeavors. Beware, for this [url=https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/QuitToMainMenu_-_Game]erases recent accomplishments and failures alike[/url].

[b]Slaughterfish Repellent:[/b] A consumable item that repels (non-frenzied) slaughterfish for a short while.

[b]Spring-Loaded Wine:[/b] Launches upward when dropped. Pushes you upward when consumed.

[b][url=https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Whimsical_Ring]Whimsical Ring[/url]:[/b] An enchanted ring. When you wear it, your attacks with a sword or greatsword will deal almost no damage, but will have a random chance to instantly kill your target.

[/spoiler]

[b]Spell Tomes:[/b]
[spoiler]

[b]Goldspill:[/b] Causes the target to drop a handful of gold.

[b]Healthbar:[/b] When cast on a target, causes a floating health meter to appear above their head.

[/spoiler]


[size=4][b]Notes[/b][/size]
This mod was created using the alpha version of DovahKit, my attempt at creating a third-party Creation Kit. My dual goals were to demonstrate things that DovahKit can do, and to demonstrate things that the vanilla game engine is capable of, without the need for SKSE or for user-side patchers like Pandora or Nemesis. (Of course, pairing the techniques used here with those tools would allow for even greater power.)

I did use the Creation Kit to edit Khahana's face and to place her in the game world, since DovahKit currently can't edit actor faces and can't place objects in the game world super easily. Absolutely all other game data was done in DovahKit: all dialogue, all items, and so on. 3D models were made using NifSkope, Blender via PyNifly, and in some cases the Creation Kit (NifSkope can't preview particle emitters, and PyNifly can't work with them at all, so the CK is the only way to check whether they look the way you want).
